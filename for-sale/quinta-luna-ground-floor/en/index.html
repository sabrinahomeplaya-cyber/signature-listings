DESCRIPTION

Imagine waking up to the sound of the sea and savoring your morning coffee with sand between your toes, all before breakfast. Welcome to Quinta Luna, an exclusive 3-bedroom oceanfront condominium that lives like a private villa in Puerto Aventuras. This spacious residence masterfully blends Spanish and Mayan design with timeless elegance.


From hand-crafted wrought iron gates to marble inlays, every detail invites you to linger, perhaps for one more margarita, until the glittering ocean view calls you back to the terrace. The primary suite offers extraordinary ocean views, a generous walk-in closet, and a shower with a distinctly romantic atmosphere.


This exceptional property features direct beach access, a covered terrace facing the sea, and a private enclosed courtyard. The living space includes a fully equipped kitchen with a bar and open-plan layout, as well as a flexible multipurpose room that can serve as a home office, storage, or a comfortable TV lounge. An outdoor bar, shower, and guest bathroom complete this perfect setting for sophisticated entertaining.
