DESCRIPTION
Nestled along a peaceful residential street within Puerto Aventuras' most coveted Marina del Rey building, this waterfront residence captures the essence of Caribbean sophistication. The southwest orientation bathes the interiors in golden afternoon light while framing spectacular marina views and sunset panoramas that paint the sky each evening. The open-concept living spaces flow seamlessly from kitchen to dining to living areas, all enhanced by floor-to-ceiling glass doors that dissolve the boundaries between indoor and outdoor living. Two generously appointed bedrooms each feature walk-in closets and private bathrooms, while thoughtful details like the powder room and dedicated laundry area with washer and dryer add practical luxury to daily life. The expansive covered terrace becomes your private viewing deck for the marina's daily rhythm, while the building's meticulously maintained gardens and swimming pool area provide additional spaces for relaxation. A backup generator ensures uninterrupted comfort, while dedicated parking for both your vehicle and golf cart speaks to the effortless lifestyle this residence enables. Marina del Rey stands as one of Puerto Aventuras' most prestigious addresses, offering residents exclusive access to private beaches, championship golf, tennis and paddle courts, and a vibrant marina community. This turnkey residence represents not just a home, but an entry into one of the Riviera Maya's most sought-after resort communities, where Caribbean tranquility meets sophisticated amenities.
