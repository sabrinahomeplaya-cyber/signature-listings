DESCRIPTION

Nestled in the prestigious Phase 4 of Puerto Aventuras, Casa Xaman Ek is an exceptional residence offering direct marina access from its own private dock. Surrounded by cenotes and La Caleta, a natural and private beach just steps away, this villa embodies the rare balance of privacy, nature, and coastal luxury.


Spanning two levels, the property features 6,500 sq ft of construction. It offers five en-suite bedrooms, including a master suite with indoor and outdoor showers, a steam room, and a panoramic balcony overlooking the canal. The contemporary architecture optimizes natural light and space, creating environments as beautiful to live in as they are for hosting guests.


The villa is sold fully furnished and equipped: a professional-grade kitchen, indoor and outdoor SONOS audio system, solar panels, high-end appliances, and a terrace BBQ area. From your private dock, set sail at any moment. The pool, lush garden, and outdoor living spaces complete a lifestyle designed for those who appreciate yachting, golf, and pristine nature.


Puerto Aventuras is one of the most established gated communities in the Riviera Maya, offering 24/7 security, a bilingual school, a marina, restaurants, a beach club, and a golf course just minutes away. An exceptional investment in one of Mexico's most sought-after markets.
