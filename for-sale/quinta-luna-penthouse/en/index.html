DESCRIPTION

Some properties are built. This one was crafted. The Quinta Luna Penthouse is a completely one-of-a-kind residence in Puerto Aventuras, a secure gated community on the Riviera Maya, where the Caribbean Sea stretches endlessly beyond your panoramic terrace. This is not just another listing; it is an opportunity that will not come again.


This four-bedroom residence has been entirely reimagined, leaving absolutely no detail to chance. The fluid volumes of its open-concept design navigate between an epicurean kitchen, outfitted with state-of-the-art appliances and custom cabinetry, and a masterful living space that extends onto one of the largest private terraces in the complex. An imposing dining palapa elevates the exterior, designed for dining under the stars with the ocean as your backdrop.


The master suite is a true sanctuary within the property: a majestic bathtub with dual showers, twin vanities, solid wood walk-in closets, and direct access to the terrace where the whisper of the waves wakes you each morning. Three additional bedrooms, including an independent junior suite with its own private entrance, offer absolute flexibility for your family, guests, or rental yields, while the private garage completes this exceptional layout.


The residence's amenities are equally outstanding: the largest private beach in Puerto Aventuras, a fitness center, a backup generator powering the entire building, and a dedicated on-site team (manager, concierge, gardeners, and private housekeeping services). In addition, there are new water treatment systems and a reserve fund exceeding $100,000 USD. A remarkable fact for the buyer's peace of mind, property taxes and condominium fees are fully paid through 2027.


There are no compromises here: this is the very essence of the Riviera Maya at its finest.
