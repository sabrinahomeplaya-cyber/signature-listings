DESCRIPTION

Some properties are built. This one was crafted. Quinta Luna Penthouse is a completely one-of-a-kind residence in Puerto Aventuras, a gated marina community on the Riviera Maya, where the Caribbean stretches endlessly beyond your wrap-around terrace. This is not a listing, it is an opportunity that will not come again.


The four-bedroom residence was reimagined from the ground up, with no detail left to chance. The open-concept layout flows between a gourmet kitchen, outfitted with new appliances and bespoke cabinetry, and a sweeping living area that opens onto one of the largest private terraces in the complex. A monumental outdoor dining palapa anchors the exterior, designed to host under the stars with the ocean as your backdrop.


The primary suite is a sanctuary within a sanctuary: dual showers, dual sinks, generous walk-in closets, and direct access to the terrace, where the sound of the sea greets you each morning. Three additional bedrooms, including a self-contained junior suite with its own entrance, offer exceptional flexibility for family, guests, or rental use, and its private garage completes this rare configuration.


The building's amenities are equally rare: the largest private beach in Puerto Aventuras, a gym, a full backup generator powering the entire building, on-site staff including an administrator, maintenance team, gardener, and cleaning crew, new water treatment systems throughout, and a reserve fund exceeding $100,000 USD. Property taxes and HOA fees are fully paid through 2027. This is not a compromise, it is the Riviera Maya at its finest.
