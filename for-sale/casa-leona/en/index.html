DESCRIPTION

Casa Leona is a residence of absolute rarity, nestled in the heart of Puerto Aventuras. What fundamentally distinguishes it from any other property on the market is not simply its location or volumes, but the intrinsic quality of its materials, selected with a standard rarely encountered in the Riviera Maya.


Each surface tells a story of craftsmanship and authenticity. Hand-cut natural stone floors, exposed stone walls sculpted with artisanal precision, and vaulted brick ceilings that create unique acoustics and warmth—everything here has been designed to last for generations. Solid woodwork, wrought iron details, and architectural elements in local limestone give the property a rare colonial character, halfway between a Mexican hacienda and a Mediterranean estate.


The two levels of the residence unfold across 14,000 square feet of construction around generous reception spaces with exceptional ceiling heights. The prestigious dining room, framed by stone arches and illuminated by a monumental wrought iron chandelier, and the integrated wine cellar with its stone colonnades, each room has its own identity—an architectural signature that can neither be copied nor reproduced.


Completing this material excellence, 30 solar panels ensure exemplary energy autonomy, combining luxury with responsibility. Direct beach access, a private pool, solarium, and 24/7 security complete a unique proposal on the Puerto Aventuras market: a home that is not only beautiful, but precious.
