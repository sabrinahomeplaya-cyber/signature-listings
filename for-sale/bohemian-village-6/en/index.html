Description
Nestled along the serene Bahía Chemuyil street within the exclusive Bohemian Village, this contemporary townhouse captures the essence of refined Caribbean living. The fairway stretches endlessly beyond your windows, creating a verdant backdrop that shifts with the golden light throughout the day. Here, the gentle rhythm of golf carts and distant ocean breezes establishes a tranquil sanctuary where time moves at your chosen pace. The residence unfolds across two thoughtfully designed levels, where natural light cascades through every room and cross ventilation carries the Caribbean air throughout. The main floor embraces an open concept living and dining area that flows seamlessly onto a private terrace, where the pool beckons and tropical gardens frame intimate moments. The equipped kitchen and independent laundry room provide modern convenience, while a powder room accommodates guests with understated elegance. Upstairs, three bedrooms and three full bathrooms create private retreats for family and friends. The master suite offers a sanctuary complete with ensuite bathroom and walk in closet, while secondary bedrooms provide comfortable havens overlooking the lush surroundings. The outdoor living extends to accommodate three vehicles and a golf cart, with both covered and open parking areas that blend functionality with the tropical aesthetic. Bohemian Village delivers an elevated lifestyle within Puerto Aventuras' secure embrace, where the lap pool with swimming lane, expansive green spaces, and palapa gathering areas create community connections. This turnkey opportunity, delivered unfurnished for personal expression, represents both a permanent residence and investment potential in one of the Riviera Maya's most coveted enclaves, where the marina lifestyle meets championship golf.
