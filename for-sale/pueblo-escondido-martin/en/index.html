DESCRIPTION
Embrace the pinnacle of coastal elegance within the exclusive gates of Puerto Aventuras. Situated in the highly prestigious Pueblo Escondido development, this exceptional beachfront residence offers a seamless blend of natural beauty and refined architecture, perfectly framing the endless blues of the Caribbean Sea. Spanning a generous 2,691 square feet of total living space, the home features 2,110 square feet of impeccably designed interiors. The layout effortlessly connects three spacious bedrooms and three-and-a-half bathrooms, ensuring absolute comfort and privacy. The true essence of this property, however, lies in its fluid outdoor living experience. Three distinct private terraces invite different moments throughout the day: a majestic oceanfront terrace complete with a private jacuzzi, a serene space overlooking lush tropical gardens, and an elevated rooftop-style terrace curated to capture spectacular Caribbean sunsets. Delivered fully furnished and completely turnkey, the residence is appointed with high-end finishes, including pristine marble flooring, custom fine-wood cabinetry, and a fully equipped chef's kitchen. Whether envisioned as a permanent coastal haven or a high-yielding luxury vacation rental, the property is primed to deliver immediate value without any need for updates. The Pueblo Escondido lifestyle is defined by its world-class, resort-style amenities. Residents enjoy exclusive access to natural seawater pools, a stunning oceanfront swimming pool, an additional rooftop pool, a state-of-the-art fitness center, and direct access to the sea. All of this is safeguarded by 24/7 controlled access and security, offering profound peace of mind. The property includes an assigned parking space. A monthly maintenance fee of $14,600 MXN covers the pristine upkeep of both the condominium and the Puerto Aventuras master community, with highly attractive annual property taxes of just $6,200 MXN.
